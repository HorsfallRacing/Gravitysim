Corners

Location, direction and radius come from GPS heading/curvature, not speed. Grip limit and predicted apex speed come from a forward (coast) / backward (max-brake) envelope pass — see main description above. Actual apex speed is shown purely as a validation reference. Load transfer is exact rigid-body statics (weight × lateral g × CofG height ÷ track) at the predicted apex — it shows how much load moves to the outside tyres, not how much grip that costs; no tyre load-sensitivity curve is assumed, so don't read a speed penalty into it.

⚠ Grip limits, apex speeds and braking figures below are model predictions only — indicative, untested against real braking, and not verified for safety. Do not rely on or act upon them.
