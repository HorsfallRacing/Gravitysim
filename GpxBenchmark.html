Corners & Sectors

One row per corner, and one sector per corner: the approach plus the corner itself, bounded at corner exits. Every metre belongs to exactly one sector, so the six sector times sum to Run Time. Sorted by Δt — time lost or gained — rather than by speed error, since a large km/h miss through a short corner can cost less than a small one down a long sector. Location, direction and radius come from GPS heading/curvature; grip limit and predicted apex from the forward/backward envelope pass. Actual apex and actual sector time are validation references and feed nothing back into the model. Click a row for load transfer.

Actual sector times are read from the selected run's GPX clock (picked below, under Route Map), not integrated from its speed trace. Timestamps are whole seconds, so a sector boundary falling between two trackpoints carries roughly ±0.5s — treat Δt under about half a second as agreement, not as a finding.

⚠ Grip limits, apex speeds and braking figures above are model predictions only — indicative, untested against real braking, and not verified for safety. Do not rely on or act upon them.

Model vs Reality by Section

RMSE = Root Mean Square Error, worst section first. Lower = better fit. Δ = Actual – Predicted (positive = model is conservative).

Route Map

Colour the route by predicted speed (updates live with the parameters above), by one run's actual recorded speed, or by Δ actual − predicted — the three share one colour scale so switching between them is a like-for-like read. Named corners highlighted · braking zones marked. Map tiles require an internet connection — everything else on this page works offline.

Actual and Δ are drawn along the selected run's own GPS line, not the canonical route, so the line you see is the line that run took. Distance-along-track differs between runs (total lengths span 1072–1112m), so Δ against distance carries that same drift — treat it as ±1–2% on the x-axis, not exact.
